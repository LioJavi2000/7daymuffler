# Custom Cabinet — Design & Vibe Skill

## Style Identity
**Architectural Minimalist Luxury (Red / Black / White)**

---

## Visual Core

The design must follow a high-end, editorial aesthetic inspired by Architectural Digest and luxury portfolio websites. The color palette is strictly limited to:

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Primary Background / Dark Base | Deep Onyx Black | `#000000` |
| Primary Text / Light Contrast | Pure Stark White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Strategic Accents & CTAs Only | Crimson Red | `#E63946` |

---

## Typography & Hierarchy

### Primary Header
- **Font:** High-contrast Serif (e.g., Playfair Display)
- **Style:** Heavy italics for a "couture" look

### Body & Metadata
- **Font:** Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter)
- **Letter Spacing:** Extra-wide (`0.3em`) for navigation and sub-headers to convey premium status

### Heading Style
- **Size:** Oversized typography (`7xl` to `9xl`)
- **Line Height:** Tight leading (`0.9`) to create a "cinematographic" impact

---

## Layout & UI Components

### Glassmorphism
Use subtle `backdrop-blur` effects (`bg-white/10`) for navigation bars.

### Bento Grid
Use an asymmetrical grid layout for galleries where items vary in height and width, creating a modern "tiled" look.

### Hero Section
- Dark mode by default
- Background images must have a **60% black overlay** or a **linear-to-bottom black gradient** to ensure text readability and a "moody" atmosphere

---

## Accent Rules

Red (`#E63946`) is reserved **exclusively** for:

- Thin structural lines (divider bars)
- Numerical labels (`01`, `02`, `03`)
- Main action buttons — **square corners only, no rounded edges** for a sharper, more aggressive feel
- Keywords that represent "action" or "perfection"

> ⚠️ Do not use red for backgrounds, large fills, or decorative elements.

---

## Photography Philosophy

Images should be **desaturated or high-contrast**. Focus on:

- **Macro shots** of materials — wood grain, marble, steel
- **Wide-angle architectural shots** of finished spaces

**Avoid:** bright, cluttered, or "homey" photos.
**Everything must look:** sterile, expensive, and precise.
