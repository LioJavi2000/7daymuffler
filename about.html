7 Day Muffler & Auto Repair has been a staple of the Stanton, CA community since 1988. What started as a small family shop has grown into one of Orange County's most trusted exhaust specialists — and it's been Raffi and Peter behind the wrenches the whole time.

We believe in doing the job right the first time. No upsells, no surprises — just honest diagnostics, quality parts, and fast turnaround. When you bring your car to us, you're talking to the owners directly.
